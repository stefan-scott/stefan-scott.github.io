# Reeborg Lessons 15-20

**Points:** 23.0
**Submission type:** online upload
**Allowed file types:** py,zip,png

You will be completing the **learning steps** listed on the [CS20 Open Textbook page.](http://www.cs20.ca) The first four steps will get you started in understanding what you are going to be doing. Once you reach step 5 and beyond, put your hand up when you complete the step and I'll come double check that you've got everything done correctly. Each step will be graded in this fashion.

Make sure to save each step (CTRL-S) and organize them on your student folder. If I'm not free to check a step right when you complete it, *duplicate your tab* (Right-click on the Reeborg browser tab → duplicate) so that you can move ahead while still keeping the steps open that still need to be checked.

Each exercise from step 5 and on will be graded as either:  **Complete** (full marks),  **Partially Complete** (half marks), or **Incomplete** (no marks). You may re-work and re-check any steps that aren't fully complete.

Some steps are more difficult, and as such are given more weight in the grading matrix. The breakdown is as follows:

|  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Step Number** | **Total Marks** | **Full Marks Criteria** |
| Step 15 | 4 | Works wherever I put the windows |
| Step 16 | 3 | Psuedo-code correctly implemented in Reeborg |
| Step 17 | 5 | Correctly works no matter where Reeborg the flag are |
| Step 18 | 5 | All harvested carrots delivered to correct corner, and Reeborg turns off in correct corner |
| Step 19 | 3 | Reeborg can successfully navigate the irregularly-shaped lake. |
| Step 20 | 3 | Reeborg can close the windows of the irregularly-shaped room. |
| **Total** | **23** |  |
