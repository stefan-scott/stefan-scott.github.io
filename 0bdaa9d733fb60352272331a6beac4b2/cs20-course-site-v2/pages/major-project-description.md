# Major Project Description

To wrap up our time with Computer Science, you'll get to create an open and creative final project, to demonstrate the skills you've developed throughout the course.

The goal of this project is to choose a topic related to computer science that is of interest to you, and **create the best product you can**.

You have the choice to either work on the project by yourself, or team up with one other person interested in the same topic.  You should realize, however, that if you team up with another person, you will both get the same final grade, AND the project will be expected to take twice the amount of work than if you were by yourself. You must also clearly explain which parts of the project each of you are responsible for.

Here is a list of really interesting topics previously chosen by students. This list is provided just to get your creative juices flowing -- feel free to do something similar to one of these, or something completely different!

- creating a game [using something like GameMaker, Unity, Kodu, Scratch, pyGame]
- making a font out of your handwriting, then using it to typeset a comic strip
- producing an interactive Mandelbrot set fractal viewer for Xbox (using C# and XNA)
- designing and developing a website for a local business
- creating a fully functional computer housed in a pizza box (power supply in an attached breadsticks box)
- developing a software calculator with customized features
- writing an essay on a topic of interest in computer science or information technology
- ???

Final Considerations:

- I will give you as much class time as I can to working on your projects, with much of the time coming in the later portion of the semester. Feel free to bring whatever devices/materials you need to work on the project in class.
- We will devote the final week of the semester to presenting your projects.
